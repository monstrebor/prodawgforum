4. point_transactions ⭐⭐⭐

This is very important for your particular idea.

Every time someone gives or removes Life Points, record it.

point_transactions
--------------------------------
id
from_user_id
to_user_id
amount
type
reason
created_at

Example:

from_user_id: 5
to_user_id: 10
amount: 1
type: give
reason: helpful

Another:

from_user_id: 7
to_user_id: 10
amount: -2
type: attack
reason: penalty

So if Alice attacks Bob:

Alice → Bob
-2 ❤️

The transaction gets saved.

This gives you a complete history.

5. Why transactions are important

Suppose Bob suddenly has:

❤️ 0

You need to know:

Who caused Bob to lose his points?

You can inspect:

point_transactions

and see:

Alice → Bob    -2
John → Bob     -2
Mike → Bob     -2
Sarah → Bob    -2

You can also display something like:

Recent Activity

⚔️ Alice attacked you       -2 ❤️
❤️ Sarah revived you        +1 ❤️
⚔️ John attacked you        -2 ❤️

This will make the game feel much more alive.

6. player_actions

This handles restrictions on actions.

For example, you don't want someone doing this:

Attack Bob
Attack Bob
Attack Bob
Attack Bob
Attack Bob
...

You could have:

player_actions
--------------------------------
id
user_id
target_user_id
action_type
amount
created_at

For example:

user_id: 5
target_user_id: 10
action_type: attack
amount: -2
created_at: ...

Then your application can enforce rules such as:

Attack:
1 time per target every 24 hours

or:

Give:
5 times per day
