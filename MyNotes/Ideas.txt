Yes — and I think the point system should be the foundation, while the RPG/MMORPG layer is built around it.

What you're describing is actually an interesting social-game mechanic:

Users have a limited amount of reputation/life points. Other users can either reward them or damage them. If their points reach 0, their account becomes inactive/frozen until another user revives them.

The important part is designing the rules so people can't easily abuse the system.

A possible core system

Each user has something like:

⚡ Life Points: 10

Other users can perform:

+1 point → give another user 1 point
-2 points → attack/remove 2 points from another user
When points reach 0 → Frozen
A different user can give +1 → account becomes Active again

For example:

Alice       ⚡ 10
Bob         ⚡ 7
Charlie     ⚡ 2
David       ⚡ 0  ❄️ FROZEN

Bob gives David +1:

David       ⚡ 1  🟢 ACTIVE

Or Charlie attacks Bob:

Bob: 7 → 5
But I'd add RPG mechanics around this

Instead of making "points" the entire RPG, separate the concepts:

1. ❤️ Life / Reputation

This is the system you're describing.

❤️ HP / Reputation

It determines whether the user's account is alive/active.

2. ⭐ Experience

Users gain XP from normal social activity:

Post       +10 XP
Comment    +2 XP
Like       +1 XP
Daily login +5 XP
Complete quest +50 XP

XP gives them levels.

Level 1
Level 2
Level 3
...
Level 50
3. 🪙 Currency

Have a separate currency that users can earn/spend.

For example:

🪙 Gold: 1,250

They could buy:

profile cosmetics
titles
badges
avatars
profile themes
emotes
virtual items
4. ⚔️ PvP / Social Combat

This is where your +1 and -2 mechanic becomes interesting.

A user could have:

⚔️ ATTACK
❤️ HEAL
🎁 GIVE
🛡️ DEFEND

For example:

Attack Robert

Robert loses 2 Life Points.

Heal Robert

Robert gains 1 Life Point.

That makes the social network feel like a game rather than simply a website with a points counter.

That starts looking much more like an MMORPG/social RPG.

However, there's one BIG problem with +1 / -2

Suppose I have 10 points.

You can attack me:

10 → 8 → 6 → 4 → 2 → 0

Five people can kill my account.

But I can only recover:

0 → 1 → 2 → 3...

That can become extremely brutal.

Worse, imagine a group of users targeting one person.

So I'd introduce daily action limits.

For example:

Give +1
1 time per user per day

Attack -2
1 time per user per day

And possibly:

You cannot attack the same person again for 24 hours.

That prevents someone from repeatedly destroying another account.

Even better: make attacks cost something

This is where the RPG part gets much more interesting.

Instead of:

"I can attack anyone for free."

Make attacks consume Energy.

For example:

⚡ Energy: 8/10

Attack → costs 2 Energy
Heal   → costs 1 Energy

Now users have to decide how they spend their actions.

Example:

Alice

❤️ 8 Life
⚡ 6 Energy
⭐ Level 12
🪙 850 Gold

Alice attacks Bob:

Alice
Energy: 6 → 4

Bob
Life: 7 → 5

Now there's an actual game loop.

And then you can introduce classes

This is where the MMORPG concept can start becoming clearer.

Users could eventually choose a class:

⚔️ Warrior

Good at attacking.

Defense: high
Healing: low
🧙 Mage

Special abilities.

Magic attack
Area attacks
Debuffs
❤️ Priest

🗡️ Rogue

Social/PvP-oriented.

Critical attack
Steal gold

You don't need to implement these initially.

Start with the point system first.

The "Frozen Account" idea is particularly interesting

I'd turn this into an actual RPG state.

❤️ 10-6   Healthy
❤️ 5-3    Injured
❤️ 2-1    Critical
❤️ 0      Frozen

When frozen:

┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ ❄️ CHARACTER FROZEN         │
│                             │
│ Your Life Points reached 0. │
│                             │
│ Another player must revive  │
│ you to return to the world. │
│                             │

│                             │

└─────────────────────────────┘

And your friends might see:

David has fallen! ❄️

Then someone can click:

[ Revive David +1 ]

That is much more memorable than simply saying "account disabled."

I'd also make "revival" social

This could become one of the strongest parts of the game.

When someone is frozen:

David has fallen.

You have 3 Revival Tokens.

Perhaps users who frequently help others receive a title:

🏆 Guardian
🏆 Healer

Now being a helpful member of the social network has gameplay value.
